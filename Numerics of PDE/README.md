\# Numerics of PDE



This folder contains MATLAB implementations for numerical solutions of Partial Differential Equations (PDEs).



The codes focus on classical numerical techniques for solving elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic PDEs.



Implemented methods include:



\- Finite Difference Methods (FDM)

\- Explicit and implicit finite difference schemes

\- Heat equation solvers

\- Wave equation solvers

\- Elliptic boundary value problem solvers

\- Stability and convergence analysis of numerical schemes



These MATLAB implementations were developed as part of the Numerics of Partial Differential Equations coursework of the M.Sc. Applied Mathematics (2 years) programme at:



South Asian University (SAU), New Delhi, India
